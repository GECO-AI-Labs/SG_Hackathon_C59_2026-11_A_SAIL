SAIL 8-Factor Mathematical Model & Methodology

The SAIL Deterministic Engine computes capacity requirements, vessel economics, three-layer commitment allocations, and strategic posture directly from the 8 core scenario planning sliders: Demand growth, Freight rate level, Fuel / bunker price, Charter rate, Disruption severity, Competitor capacity, Growth ambition, and Budget ceiling.

1. Demand Growth, Growth Ambition & Competitor Capacity

Initial carried demand is anchored to baseline fleet capacity (160,000 TEU) and utilisation:
Initial Demand = Current Capacity × (Existing Utilisation / 100)
Over the planning horizon Y = 3 years, the effective compound growth rate blends Demand growth (g_mkt) and Growth ambition (g_amb), while Disruption severity (D) adds schedule absorption demand:
g_base = g_mkt + 0.65 × (g_amb - g_mkt)
Base Demand = Initial Demand × (1 + g_base)^Y × (1 + 0.08 × D)
• Low Case Growth: min(g_mkt - 3.5%, 0.60 × g_base) - 0.15 × max(0, Competitor_Capacity)
• High Case Growth: max(g_mkt + 3.5%, 1.15 × g_amb, 1.25 × g_base) + 0.04 × D

2. Disruption Severity & Required Fleet Capacity

Port congestion, canal rerouting, and schedule delays (Disruption severity D ∈ [0, 1]) extend round-trip voyage turnaround days by 1 + 0.30 × D, reducing annual TEU productivity per vessel:
Effective Vessel Productivity = Benchmark Productivity / (1 + 0.30 × D)
Required fleet capacity is buffered at an 88% target operating load:
Required Capacity = [Forecast Demand × (1 + Buffer%)] / 0.88
Required Vessels = ceil(Required Capacity / Effective Vessel Productivity)

3. Dynamic Vessel Economics: Fuel / Bunker Price, Charter Rate & Freight Rate Level

Unit vessel operating cost adjusts dynamically from Fuel / bunker price (P_bunker, $/tonne) and Charter rate (R_charter, $/day) across 330 operating days/year:
Annual Fuel Cost / Vessel = P_bunker × Daily_Fuel_Tonnes × 330
Annual Charter Cost / Vessel = R_charter × 330
Dynamic Unit OpCost = Class_Base_OpCost + ΔFuel_Cost + 0.60 × ΔCharter_Cost
Freight rate level (F_idx, % of $950/TEU baseline) drives revenue, net contribution, and break-even index:
Annual Revenue = Base Demand × $950 × (F_idx / 100)
Net Fleet Contribution = Annual Revenue - Total Annual Fleet Cost
Break-Even Freight Index = [Total Fleet Cost / (Base Demand × $950)] × 100%

4. Three-Layer Commitment Reconciliation & Budget Ceiling Governance

Unconstrained incremental requirements are split across Low, Base, and High cases, then governed by the Budget ceiling (B_max) and market risk gates:
• Max Affordable Incremental TEU: (Budget Ceiling / Dynamic Unit OpCost) × Effective Productivity
• Locked Capacity: min(Raw_Locked × Risk_Gate, Max_Affordable_TEU) (reduced when Freight Rate Level < 88% or Disruption > 45%)
• Flexible Capacity: min(Desired_Base_Incremental - Locked, Remaining_Budget_TEU)
• Watch Zone Capacity: Raw_Watch_Zone + Budget_Deferred_Base_TEU
Reconciliation Identity: Current Fleet + Locked + Flexible + Watch Zone = Total High Requirement (100%).

5. Multi-Signal Rule-Based Posture Engine

Synthesizes all 8 sliders simultaneously to output:
• Aggressive Growth: High demand growth (≥8%) & ambition (≥10%), freight rate ≥95%, low competitor additions (≤6%), and incremental cost within Budget ceiling.
• Defensive Growth: Aggressive competitor capacity expansion (≥8% or >Demand+4%) threatening route share.
• Capacity Preservation: Weak/negative demand (<0.5%), depressed freight rate (<82%), or high break-even cost squeeze.
• Flexible Expansion: Elevated disruption severity (≥35%), binding budget ceiling, or uncertain macro conditions favoring modular charters and Watch Zone options.
